Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Dumitru Bumbu ("we", "us", or "our") built Color Sort ("the App") as a free game supported by ads, with an optional one-time purchase to remove them. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect any personal information. We do not require you to create an account, or to provide your name, email address, or any other personal data to use the App.

All game data (level progress, statistics, preferences) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. We do not operate a server or backend for this App.

Third-Party Advertising

The free version of the App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect certain data in order to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device Identifier — Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for ad targeting.
  • Coarse Location — Approximate location based on IP address for regional ad relevance.
  • Usage Data — Information about how you interact with ads (impressions, clicks).
  • Advertising Data — Information about advertisements displayed to you.

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps.

Consent and Your Choices in Europe

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App presents a consent form before any personalized advertising is shown, and you may consent or decline. You may also limit ad tracking at any time from your device settings.

On iOS, the App also asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used for tracking. You may decline; the game works exactly the same either way, and you can change this later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Game Center

The App offers an optional Game Center leaderboard for your highest completed level. If you choose to sign in, your score and Game Center identity are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Game Center is entirely optional and the App is fully playable without it.

In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time purchase to remove advertising. All payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App contains third-party advertising which may collect device identifiers as described above.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to retain. Game data stored locally on your device persists until you delete the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
