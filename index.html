How to play

Tap a tube to lift its top colour, then tap another tube to pour it in. A colour will only pour onto the same colour, or into empty space.

Clear the level by gathering every colour into a tube of its own. If you get stuck, undo as many moves as you need — or restart the level and try a different order.
