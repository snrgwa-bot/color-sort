Common questions

How do I remove the ads?
Open Settings inside the app and choose Remove Ads. It is a one-time purchase.

I bought Remove Ads but the ads came back.
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you purchased with.

How do I reset my progress?
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears all locally stored progress.
