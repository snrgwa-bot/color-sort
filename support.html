Common questions

How do I remove the ads?
Open Settings inside the app and choose Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase — no subscription.

I bought Remove Ads but they came back.
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I play without the internet?
Yes. Every level runs on your device. You'll only see ads when you're online.

How do I reset my progress?
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears all locally stored progress. There's no way to undo that, so be sure.

The leaderboard is empty.
Game Center is optional — sign in from the iOS Settings app under Game Center, then reopen Color Sort.
