Common questions

How do I remove the ads?
Open Settings inside the app and choose Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase — there is no subscription and nothing renews.

I bought Remove Ads but they came back.
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I play without the internet?
Yes. Every level runs on your device. You'll only see ads while you're online.

I'm colour blind — can I still play?
Yes. Turn on Colour Blind Symbols in Settings and every colour gets its own shape. It also switches on automatically if you use iOS's Differentiate Without Colour.

How do I reset my progress?
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears all locally stored progress. That can't be undone, so be sure.

The leaderboard is empty.
Game Center is optional — sign in from the iOS Settings app under Game Center, then reopen Color Sort.
